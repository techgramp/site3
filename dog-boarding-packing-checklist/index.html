Local information from Woodlands Pet Retreat

Whether you are boarding your dog during work travel, a Camden County weekend, or a Cumberland Island day trip, a little preparation makes the handoff easier for you and your pet.

Use this checklist for general planning, then confirm Woodlands Pet Retreat's current intake instructions for your specific booking. Individual dogs can have different needs, especially when medications, allergies or special feeding schedules are involved.

Before drop-off

Bring the important information

  • Health records: Confirm your dog's required Rabies, Bordetella and DHLPP vaccinations are current and that the facility has documentation.
  • Food: Have clear feeding instructions, portions, food sensitivities and treats identified.
  • Medications: Share labels, prescribed directions and emergency instructions. Ask the facility what it can administer.
  • Contact details: Supply your phone number, a reachable backup person and veterinarian information.
  • Behavior and routine: Explain any anxiety, triggers, usual rest schedule or special handling preferences.
  • Pickup plans: Confirm pickup/drop-off windows and who is authorized to collect your dog.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Which vaccinations does Woodlands list?

The existing Woodlands information lists current Rabies, Bordetella and DHLPP records. Verify current acceptance requirements with the business.

Should I bring my dog’s medication?

Tell the facility about any medication in advance and confirm that it can accommodate the prescribed routine; provide accurate labeled instructions.

What if my vacation includes the Cumberland Island ferry?

The passenger ferry does not transport pets, so confirm dog care before you finalize your travel itinerary.
