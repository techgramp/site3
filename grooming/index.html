Dog Grooming & Boarding Together

Provided by our sister salon, Chien d'amour

Home / Grooming

Grooming is handled by our sister company, Chien d'amour, Amber's boutique salon in St. Marys. Boarding guests can often have a bath or a full groom scheduled around their stay, so they go home clean instead of smelling like a kennel.

Grooming with a boarding stay

Departure bath

Added to a boarding stay so your dog is washed before pickup. Priced by size and coat condition, so ask when you book.

Full groom before pickup

A complete groom scheduled near the end of a stay. Full‑service grooming starts at $80 depending on your dog's size.

Brushing during the stay

$10 a day to keep a longer coat from matting over a multi‑night stay. Add it alongside any boarding booking.

Scheduling

Grooming with boarding depends on salon availability, so mention it when you book the stay rather than at drop‑off.

The Fresh‑Home Package bundles boarding with a daily photo update and a departure bath, and the Deluxe Retreat adds departure grooming.

About the salon

Chien d'amour is a low‑volume boutique salon, which means dogs are groomed one at a time rather than waiting in a line of kennels. Amber personally hires every groomer working there, and the salon was named Best Pet Groomer of Camden County in 2023, 2024 and 2025.

It sits at 121 City Smitty Dr in St. Marys, a few minutes from the retreat. You can reach the salon directly on (717) 344‑7840.
