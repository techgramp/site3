Local information from Woodlands Pet Retreat

St. Marys is the gateway to Cumberland Island and a lovely place to explore the Georgia coast. Traveling with a dog involves a few extra decisions: which outings allow pets, which require alternate care, and where to find dependable boarding if your plans keep you out all day.

We put together this locally focused guide from Woodlands Pet Retreat to make those choices easier. Start with the free visitor resources, then choose the activity and dog-care option that fits your plans.

Good to know

Some attractions are pet-friendly; others aren't

The St. Marys Waterfront Park is listed as pet-friendly, while the Cumberland Island passenger ferry does not transport pets (with separate rules for qualified service animals). Confirm current rules with each destination before you leave.

For a relaxing visit, choose the dog-friendly part of your trip first. Then arrange boarding for any long outing where your dog cannot come.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Do I need boarding for a Cumberland Island day trip?

The concession-operated passenger ferry does not accept pets. If you are taking that ferry and your dog is not a qualified service animal, arrange suitable care on the mainland.

Does Woodlands provide overnight and daytime stays?

Yes. You can compare overnight boarding and day stays and request availability before booking.

Is this an official tourism website?

No. This is an independent local guide from Woodlands Pet Retreat. We link visitors directly to official tourism and park sources for current details.
