Local information from Woodlands Pet Retreat

Families in Kingsland looking for dog boarding have a few decisions to make before reserving a stay: Is your dog more comfortable in a private suite? Do pickup windows work with your trip? Does the facility accept the health records you already have?

Woodlands Pet Retreat is located at 120 Davis Street in neighboring St. Marys, Georgia. We provide private-suite boarding and day stays for dogs, including guests from Kingsland and other Camden County communities. Here is how to assess whether we are a good fit.

Compare with confidence

What matters when choosing boarding

Comfort

Private resting space

Ask how dogs are housed, how frequently they have outdoor breaks, and whether animals from unrelated households are combined. Woodlands uses private indoor suites and scheduled care.

Routine

Convenient handoffs

Check actual pickup and drop-off windows, not just advertised hours. Woodlands currently lists three daily handoff periods; book only after the timing works for your trip.

Preparation

Health and feeding records

Vaccination requirements and routine details matter. Prepare your dog's feeding information and veterinary records before the reservation date.
