Chien d'amour is a low‑volume grooming salon in St. Marys. Dogs are groomed one at a time rather than waiting in a line of kennels, which keeps the whole appointment calmer and means your dog has a single groomer's full attention from bath to bow.

How an appointment works

1

Booking and drop‑off

Book online through MoeGo and pick a time that suits you. At drop‑off we talk through the style you want, your dog's coat and anything we should know about their temperament or sore spots.

2

Bath and dry

A thorough bath with products chosen for your dog's coat and skin, then a proper fluff dry. Getting the coat genuinely clean and fully dry is what makes the finished haircut sit correctly.

3

Nails and ears

Nails trimmed and ears cleaned as part of every full groom and every bath package, not treated as an upsell.

4

The haircut

Breed‑appropriate or styled however you prefer. If you are not sure what suits your dog's coat and your grooming routine at home, say so and we will suggest something practical.

5

Finish and pickup

A final tidy, a bow or bandana if you would like one, and a quick chat at pickup about how the appointment went and when to book next.

What makes this different

One dog at a time

No crowded salon, no chorus of dryers and barking. Nervous, senior and first‑time dogs usually cope far better in this setting.

Experienced professionals

Every groomer here is an experienced professional, personally hired by Amber and held to the same standard of patient, careful handling.

Competition‑ring standards

Amber's background includes more than 10 years across grooming, dog services and dog shows, and the salon has been named Best Pet Groomer of Camden County three years running.

Quality products

Shampoos and finishing products are chosen for each dog's coat type and skin rather than one bottle for every dog through the door.

Honest advice

If a shorter cut is kinder than trying to save a badly matted coat, we will tell you and explain why, rather than putting your dog through hours of dematting.

Regulars save

Book three or more grooms within the same 60‑day period and each appointment is 10% off. See pricing.
