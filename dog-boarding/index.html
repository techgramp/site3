Dog Boarding in St. Marys

Provided by our sister company, Woodlands Pet Retreat

Home / Dog Boarding

Boarding is handled by our sister company, Woodlands Pet Retreat, also run by Amber. It is a small boarding facility on a quiet wooded lakefront in St. Marys, with private indoor suites and a caretaker on site overnight.

Visit Woodlands Pet Retreat How boarding works

What Woodlands offers

Overnight boarding

Private suites from $45 a night, with feeding on your dog's own schedule and outdoor breaks through the day.

Day stays

Daytime care at $25 a day for work days, appointments or contractors in the house, with your dog home the same evening.

One family at a time

Dogs are only ever grouped with their own family, never mixed with dogs from other households.

Groom before pickup

Because both businesses are Amber's, a bath or full groom here can often be scheduled near the end of a boarding stay.

Boarding and grooming together

If your dog is boarding at Woodlands and you would like them groomed before they come home, mention it when you book the stay rather than at drop‑off, since salon availability decides what is possible.
