Why Woodlands

Boarding that stays personal

Convenient for Kings Bay families

We are located in St. Marys with straightforward access from Kings Bay and Kingsland. Boarding can help during duty days, training, travel, leave or other times when your dog needs a safe place to stay.

Private suites and consistent care

Dogs stay in private indoor suites with scheduled feeding, regular outdoor breaks, secure fenced areas and individual attention. Dogs from different households are not mixed together.

Simple booking and updates

Create a Gingr profile once, request your dates online and keep your pet records together. Daily photo updates are also available as an add-on during the stay.

What to know before booking

Which military branches are included?

The 5% boarding discount is available to service members in the Army, Marine Corps, Navy, Air Force, Space Force and Coast Guard.

What vaccination records are required?

Rabies, bordetella and DHLPP need to be current. Records can be uploaded to your Gingr profile.

What are drop-off and pickup times?

Daily windows are 6:30 to 8:00 AM, 12:00 to 1:30 PM, and 4:30 to 6:00 PM.
