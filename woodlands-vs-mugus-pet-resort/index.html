Woodlands vs Mugu's Pet Resort: Comparing Two Very Different Stays

Resort amenities and group play, compared with quiet private‑suite boarding

Home / Compare / Woodlands vs Mugu's Pet Resort

Mugu's Pet Resort has been a fixture in Camden County for years, and if you have lived in St. Marys for a while you have probably heard the name. Woodlands Pet Retreat opened in 2025 with a deliberately different shape: much smaller, private suites, and no group play between unrelated dogs. This page compares the two fairly so you can pick the environment that fits your dog.

The clearest way to think about it: Mugu's is a resort‑style operation with amenities, including a pool and a dock‑diving area, and a cage‑free daycare where dogs play together. Woodlands is a quiet boarding facility on a wooded lakefront where each dog has their own suite. Those are two genuinely different products, and the better one depends on your dog's temperament.

Amenities versus quiet

Mugu's publishes a genuinely impressive list of activities: a swimming pool, a dock‑diving area, and a cage‑free daycare where dogs run and play rather than being kennelled during the day. Dock diving in particular is a real sport, and for a water‑mad retriever with energy to burn, a facility that offers it is a legitimate draw. If that describes your dog, you should weigh it heavily.

Woodlands offers none of that, and we would rather say so plainly than pretend otherwise. What we offer instead is quiet. The property is a wooded lot at the water's edge, dogs rest in private suites between outdoor breaks, and the building stays calm because we keep the numbers small and never mix households. For a dog who finds a busy resort overwhelming, the absence of amenities is the point.

Cage‑free versus private suites

These two phrases sound like they are competing on the same axis, and they are not, which confuses a lot of owners. "Cage‑free" usually means dogs are loose together in a shared space rather than crated during the day. "Private suite" means each dog has their own room. A dog can be uncrated and still be surrounded by strangers; a dog can be in a suite and still get plenty of outdoor time.

At Woodlands, dogs are not crated in the sense people usually worry about: they have their own suite with bedding and go out through the day for breaks and play. What they do not do is share space with dogs from other families. If loose group play is what you are looking for, Mugu's model gives you that and ours does not.

Worth noting that both of us require the same core vaccinations, rabies, DHLPP and bordetella, which is a good sign at any facility.

Who may prefer Woodlands

  • Dogs who are overwhelmed by busy, high‑energy facilities
  • Owners who want a private suite rather than shared space
  • Anyone who wants a person on the property overnight
  • Owners of anxious, reactive or senior dogs
  • People who value a quiet natural setting over amenities

Who may prefer Mugu's Pet Resort

  • Water‑loving dogs who would use the pool or dock diving
  • Dogs who thrive in cage‑free group play
  • Families who need cat boarding as well as dog boarding
  • Owners who want boarding, grooming and activities in one place
  • Anyone closer to Colerain Road, or needing dates we cannot cover

Mugu's may be the better fit if their amenities, cage‑free model, location or availability suit your dog and your schedule more closely.

Questions worth asking any boarding facility

These are the questions that actually reveal how a facility operates. Ask us, and ask anywhere else you are considering.

Where does my dog sleep?A private suite, a shared run, or an open room with other dogs?
How often do dogs go outside?Scheduled breaks, or only when staff have time?
Are unrelated dogs mixed together?Is group play the default, and can you opt out?
Who is present overnight?A person on site, a person on call, or cameras only?
How are dogs monitored?Cameras, in‑person checks, or both?
How are feeding instructions handled?Can you send your own food and keep their schedule?
Can medications be given?Is there a fee, and who administers them?
What happens in an emergency?Which vet, and how quickly are you contacted?
Can I tour before booking?Any facility confident in its space will say yes.
What vaccinations are required?Requirements protect every dog staying there.
What should I bring?Bedding, toys and food from home help dogs settle.
How are anxious or senior dogs handled?Is the routine adjusted, or is it the same for every dog?
