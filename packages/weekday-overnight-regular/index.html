$290 / month

Eight weekday overnight stays each month, billed monthly. That works out to about $36.25 per night.

What's included

  • Eight weekday overnight stays each month
  • Billed once a month
  • A private suite with bedding and fresh water
  • Feeding on your dog's schedule and regular outdoor breaks
  • A caretaker on site overnight

Best for: Shift workers, frequent travelers and regular weeknight commitments.

Firefighters, law enforcement officers and U.S. military members pay $275.50 with the 5% community discount.

Good to know

Drop-off and pick-up windows

6:30 to 8:00 AM, 12:00 to 1:30 PM and 4:30 to 6:00 PM, every day.

Vaccinations

Rabies, bordetella and DHLPP need to be current. Upload records to your Gingr profile or have your vet send them over.

Add-ons

Photo updates, nature walks, play sessions, brushing, medication and more can be added to any package. See the full add‑on list.

Drop-off and pick-up timing

Overnight packages follow our normal timing rule: a drop-off between 6:00 AM and 12:00 PM with pick-up after 12:01 PM on your departure day adds a day stay fee to your overnights.
