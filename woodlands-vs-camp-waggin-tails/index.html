Woodlands vs Camp Waggin Tails: Two Small, Owner‑Run Options

Comparing owner‑run boarding in St. Marys and Woodbine

Home / Compare / Woodlands vs Camp Waggin Tails

Camp Waggin Tails is a small, owner‑run boarding operation in Woodbine, north of St. Marys, and it comes up regularly when local owners ask each other for recommendations. Woodlands Pet Retreat is also small and owner run, in St. Marys itself. Of all the comparisons on this site, this is the one where the two businesses are most alike in spirit, so the honest differences come down to location, facilities and what each of us can verify about how we operate.

We want to be upfront about something: there is less public information available about Camp Waggin Tails than about the larger facilities in the area, and much of what circulates comes from neighbour recommendations rather than a published policy page. Where we cannot verify something, this page says so instead of guessing.

The most practical difference is distance

Camp Waggin Tails is on Old Still Road in Woodbine, roughly 20 to 25 minutes from central St. Marys depending on where you start. Woodlands is on Davis Street in St. Marys. If you are dropping off before an early flight or collecting after work, that round trip matters more than most people expect, especially across three drop‑off windows in a week.

If you live in Woodbine, Kingsland's north side, or off Exit 6, the maths reverses and they are plainly the more convenient choice. Convenience is a legitimate reason to pick a facility, and we would rather you make that call clearly than have us talk around it.

Both small, both owner run

What the two share is more interesting than what separates them. Both are small operations where the owners are the people caring for your dog, which usually means direct communication and consistency, the same faces every visit. Neither is a chain. If you have had a good experience with a small owner‑run facility before, you already know why people seek that out.

Where we can be specific about Woodlands: dogs stay in private indoor suites, go out through the day into fenced yards, are never grouped with dogs from other households, and have a caretaker on the property overnight alongside motion‑activated cameras. The business is insured. Drop‑off and pickup run in three daily windows. We can also coordinate grooming through Chien d'amour, our sister salon. For the equivalent details at Camp Waggin Tails, ask them directly, and use the question list below to make sure you cover the same ground with both of us.

Questions worth asking any boarding facility

These are the questions that actually reveal how a facility operates. Ask us, and ask anywhere else you are considering.

Where does my dog sleep?A private suite, a shared run, or an open room with other dogs?
How often do dogs go outside?Scheduled breaks, or only when staff have time?
Are unrelated dogs mixed together?Is group play the default, and can you opt out?
Who is present overnight?A person on site, a person on call, or cameras only?
How are dogs monitored?Cameras, in‑person checks, or both?
How are feeding instructions handled?Can you send your own food and keep their schedule?
Can medications be given?Is there a fee, and who administers them?
What happens in an emergency?Which vet, and how quickly are you contacted?
Can I tour before booking?Any facility confident in its space will say yes.
What vaccinations are required?Requirements protect every dog staying there.
What should I bring?Bedding, toys and food from home help dogs settle.
How are anxious or senior dogs handled?Is the routine adjusted, or is it the same for every dog?
