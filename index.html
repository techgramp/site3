    Common questions

    Good to know

    How do I book a grooming appointment?

    Book online through our MoeGo booking page. You'll choose a service, add your dog's details, and confirm your appointment. Prefer to talk it through? Call or text us at (717) 344‑7840.

    Do you offer one‑on‑one grooming?

    Yes. That's the whole idea behind Chien d'amour. It's a low‑volume boutique salon, so your dog receives focused, individual attention from their groomer rather than moving through a busy line of dogs.

    What does full‑service grooming include?

    A bath, fluff up, nail trim, ear cleaning, and a full haircut service, breed‑appropriate or styled the way you request. The Bath & Tidy service includes everything except the haircut.

    How is my dog's price determined?

    Starting prices are based on size. Final pricing depends on your dog's breed, coat type and condition, temperament, requested style, and the time the service requires. Matting can add time and cost, so mention your dog's coat condition when booking.
