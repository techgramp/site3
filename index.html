    Common questions

    Good to know

    How do I book a stay?

    New clients: create a profile once, then request your dates. Existing clients: log in and book. Prefer to talk it through? Call or text us at (912) 674‑4709.

    Can my dogs stay together?

    Yes, dogs from the same family can share a suite. Each additional dog in the same room is +$20 per night for boarding, or +$10 per day for day stays.

    What's included in every stay?

    A private suite with comfortable bedding, fresh water, scheduled feeding, regular outdoor breaks, individual attention, and facility monitoring. Deluxe suites are our largest at 5′×6′ and include a deluxe bed. Affection and bathroom breaks are never extra.

    What are the drop‑off and pickup hours?

    Drop‑off and pickup windows are 6:30 to 8:00 AM, 12:00 to 1:30 PM, and 4:30 to 6:00 PM.
