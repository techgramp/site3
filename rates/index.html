    What is included in every stay

    • A private suite with comfortable bedding and fresh water
    • Feeding on your dog's own schedule, using the food you send
    • Regular outdoor breaks and one‑on‑one attention
    • A caretaker on site at all times, including overnight
    • Motion‑activated camera monitoring throughout the facility
    • No deposits and no cancellation fees

    Pricing notes

    • Rates are per dog, per night or per day as listed
    • Additional dogs must be from the same household to share a suite
    • Bath and grooming pricing depends on size and coat condition
    • Estimates from the stay builder are not a final invoice
