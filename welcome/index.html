What every stay includes

A private suite

Your dog gets their own space with comfortable bedding and fresh water, not a shared run. Basic suites are 4′×6′ or 3′×6′; the deluxe suite is 5′×6′ with a deluxe bed.

Their own food, their own schedule

Send the food they already eat and we feed on their normal timing. No diet switch means no upset stomach halfway through the week.

Someone here overnight

A caretaker is always on site, including through the night, with motion‑activated cameras throughout the building.

Outdoor time, one family at a time

Fenced yards under the pines, with breaks and play through the day. Dogs are never mixed with dogs from other households.

Photo updates

Add a daily photo for $5 and we text or email pictures so you are not wondering how the day went.

Medication if needed

We are glad to give medications with clear instructions, for a $2 administration fee.

Quick answers

Is someone really there overnight?

Yes. A caretaker is always on site, including overnight, and motion‑activated cameras cover the building so we can check in any time.

Will my dog be around strange dogs?

No. This is a one‑on‑one structured facility. Dogs are only grouped with their own family.

What do I need to bring?

Their food and dishes, bed, blankets, toys and treats. Anything that helps them feel at home, within reason.

What vaccinations do you require?

Rabies, bordetella and DHLPP need to be current. Your vet can email records directly.

What if there is an emergency?

Your dog goes straight to Robinson's Animal Hospital in Yulee and we contact you as soon as possible.

Suites are limited, especially around holidays

Reserve your dates now. No deposit, and no cancellation fee if plans change.
