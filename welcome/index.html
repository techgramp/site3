Quick answers

How often should my dog be groomed?

Many coated breeds do best every 4 to 8 weeks. Short‑coated dogs can usually go longer. We will suggest a routine at your first visit.

What if my dog's coat is matted?

Matting adds time and cost, and heavy matting can be painful to brush out. Mention the coat condition when booking so we schedule enough time.

How long will it take?

Appointments are one‑on‑one, so your dog is not waiting in a kennel all day. Your groomer can give an estimate at drop‑off.

Do you groom puppies?

Yes. First appointments are kept short and positive so the experience sets a good pattern. Tell us it is a first groom when you book.
