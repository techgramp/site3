Local information from Woodlands Pet Retreat

St. Marys has waterfront views, history and access to coastal Georgia’s outdoors. Traveling with a dog can be part of the experience, especially when you choose open-air activities, bring water and respect local leash and cleanup rules.

This guide focuses on verified pet-friendly options and clear reminders about restrictions. We recommend confirming policies immediately before your visit because festivals, buildings and individual activities may have separate requirements.

Outdoor stops with your dog

Downtown

St. Marys Waterfront Park

The riverfront walkway is a pleasant way to experience downtown. Explore Georgia labels the park pet-friendly. Keep your dog under control, watch for crowding, and check event-specific restrictions.

Coastal trails are a good choice for active dogs. Georgia State Parks generally welcomes leashed dogs on permitted trails, with a six-foot leash limit and restrictions for buildings and some facilities.

Coastal comfort

A few ways to keep your pup safe

  • Carry fresh water and plan shorter walks on hot, humid days.
  • Use a leash and bring waste bags; watch for other animals and wildlife.
  • Check pavement temperature before longer afternoon walks.
  • Review park and business rules rather than assuming all indoor areas admit pets.
  • Never leave your dog alone in a parked vehicle while sightseeing.

St. Marys is also the gateway to Cumberland Island. The usual passenger ferry does not carry pets; plan appropriate mainland care for that excursion.

Questions, answered

Frequently asked questions

Is St. Marys Waterfront Park dog-friendly?

Explore Georgia lists the waterfront park as pet-friendly. Keep your pet controlled and verify any event-specific rules.

Are dogs allowed on trails at Crooked River State Park?

Georgia State Parks generally permits leashed dogs on many trails, subject to a six-foot leash rule and posted restrictions. Confirm with the park before visiting.

What can I do if my activity does not allow dogs?

Arrange dog care in advance and confirm hours and pickup timing. Woodlands offers daytime and overnight options in St. Marys.
