Frequently Asked Questions

Booking, preparing, feeding, medication, policies and pricing

Home / FAQ

Everything we get asked most often, grouped by topic. If your question is not here, call or text (912) 674‑4709 or email woodlandspetretreat@gmail.com and we will answer it directly.

Booking

How do I book a stay?

New clients create a profile once through the online Gingr portal, then request dates. Existing clients log in and book. You can also call or text (912) 674‑4709.

How far in advance should I book?

As early as possible for holidays, spring break and summer. The facility is small, so space is genuinely limited during busy periods.

Are there deposits or cancellation fees?

No deposits and no cancellation fees. We know plans change.

Can I tour the facility first?

Yes. Call or text (912) 674‑4709 or email woodlandspetretreat@gmail.com to arrange a tour. You can also see photos of the facility first.

Preparing for a stay

What should I bring?

Their food and food dishes, bed, blankets, toys and treats. Anything that helps your dog feel at home is welcome, within reason. If their routine is very specific or out of the ordinary, contact us ahead of time and we will work it out together.

What vaccinations are required?

Rabies, bordetella and DHLPP need to be current. Have records ready when you book, or ask your vet to send them over.

Is a trial visit a good idea for a nervous dog?

Often, yes. A day stay lets your dog explore and meet us without an overnight, which usually makes the first boarding stay much easier.

Feeding

How does feeding work?

Send your dog's own food and we feed on their normal schedule. Keeping their diet unchanged avoids the stomach upset that often comes with switching food during a stay.

Can you handle special diets or feeding routines?

Usually, yes. Tell us the details at booking, and contact us ahead of time if the routine is unusual so we can plan for it properly.

Medication

Can you give my dog medication?

Yes. There is a $2 administration fee. Please send medication with clear dosing instructions.

Drop-off and pickup

What are the drop-off and pickup hours?

Three windows every day: 6:30 to 8:00 AM, 12:00 to 1:30 PM, and 4:30 to 6:00 PM.

During the stay

How does overnight monitoring work?

A caretaker is always on site, including overnight. The facility also has motion-activated cameras throughout, so we can check in on the dogs at any time and respond right away if anything needs attention.

Can I see how my dog is doing?

Add a daily photo update for $5 per day to receive photos by text or email. The Gingr Pet Parent app (facility code 582844) also keeps your bookings and records handy.

How are dogs grouped or separated?

This is a one-on-one, structured facility. Dogs are only ever grouped with their own family, and dogs from different families are never mixed together.

What happens in an emergency?

Your dog is taken immediately to Robinson's Animal Hospital in Yulee, and we contact you as soon as possible.

Multiple dogs and long stays

Can my dogs stay together?

Yes, dogs from the same family can share a suite. Each additional dog is $20 per night for boarding or $10 per day for day stays. See the rates page.

Do you take longer stays?

Yes. For extended trips the packages bring the nightly cost down, and add-ons like walks and play sessions help keep longer stays interesting. Compare packages.

Day stays

What is a day stay?

Daytime care with no overnight, at $25 per day. Your dog gets a comfortable bed, outdoor breaks and individual attention, then goes home the same day. More about day stays.

Pricing

How much does boarding cost?

Basic suites are $45 per night and deluxe suites are $65 per night. Day stays are $25 per day. The rates page has add-ons and packages.

Do you offer grooming?

Grooming is handled by our sister company Chien d'amour, and a departure bath or groom can be coordinated with a boarding stay when scheduling allows.

Ready to book their stay?

Suites fill up around holidays and school breaks. Reserve your dates early.
