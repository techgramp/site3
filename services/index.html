What affects the final price

  • Size and breed — more coat takes more time
  • Coat condition — matting adds time, and heavy matting may mean a shorter cut
  • Style requested — hand scissoring takes longer than a clipper cut
  • Temperament — anxious dogs are worked at their own pace, with breaks
  • Time since the last groom — longer gaps usually mean more work

Mention your dog's coat condition when you book so the appointment is scheduled with enough time. More detail in the FAQ.
